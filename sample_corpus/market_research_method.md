# Market research method

A market price is the clearing signal for expected marginal supply and demand, not a direct score for one fundamental indicator. Research should separate observed conditions from expectations and identify the time horizon of each claim.

Useful evidence includes official statistics, company disclosures, system operator data, credible reporting, and well-attributed analysis. Conflicting evidence is not automatically an error: two signals may operate at different horizons or describe different parts of a market balance.

Strong market conclusions normally require multiple independent sources, recent observations, and a plausible transmission mechanism. When these are missing, evidence strength should remain low and the brief should state what data would change the view.
